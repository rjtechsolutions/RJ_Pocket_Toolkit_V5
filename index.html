ℹ️ About RJ Pocket Toolkit

RJ Pocket Toolkit is a business utility for creating invoices, quotations, managing customers and items, and using common business calculators.

Publisher / Developer: RJ TECH Solutions • Pune, Maharashtra

Current release: Free Launch Edition

Why is it free? RJ Pocket Toolkit is currently offered free to everyone during its initial launch period. We are opening the app to users for the first 2–3 months so we can understand real-world usage, collect feedback, improve reliability and make the product better before introducing any paid plans.

Launch policy: There is currently no payment requirement for the available app features. Future pricing or subscription changes, if introduced, will be communicated clearly before they take effect.

Account: A login is required so your cloud data can remain separated by account and synchronized across supported devices.

🔐 Privacy & Data Policy

What we store: account email or supported login identity, business settings, invoices, quotations, customer details, item/service details and app preferences that you choose to save.

Why we store it: to provide login, cloud synchronization, invoice/quotation history and backup/restore features.

Where data is stored: the app can keep working data in browser/app storage and synchronizes your account data to the configured Supabase cloud database. Access is designed to be restricted to the authenticated account.

Your responsibility: do not enter information you are not authorized to store. Before deleting browser/app data, changing devices, or uninstalling the app, export a backup.

Data deletion: if you want your account/cloud data deleted, contact the publisher using the support/business contact provided in the app. Deletion requests may require account verification.

Third parties: authentication, email/SMS delivery, Google sign-in and cloud database services may use configured third-party infrastructure. Their service availability and policies may apply.

Security: the app uses authenticated access and database-level user isolation. No online system can guarantee absolute security, so keep your login credentials private and maintain your own backup.
